About street cams in Japan

These 63 feeds come from 33 channels, led by 京都観光混雑状況Live (11), Kabukicho Live Camera (7), and 北陸朝日放送公式チャンネル (3) — a mix of operators running multiple cameras and one-off contributors, which is why framing varies across the page.

Street makes up 9% of the 726 cameras Camlisted currently tracks in Japan, so this page is a slice of that catalogue rather than the whole of it.

Japan runs on UTC+09:00, so the overnight stretch that yields low-light footage falls roughly between 13:00 and 20:00 UTC — worth knowing if you are after night scenes rather than daylight.

Resolution figures here are measured, not scraped from titles: Camlisted records the real playback quality when a feed is opened. 19 of these feeds have been measured so far — 2 in 4K, 3 in QHD, 12 in full HD, and 2 in HD.

The longest-running live camera in this set has been broadcasting continuously since 2020 — “【LIVE】歌舞伎町ライブカメラ Shinjuku Kabukicho Live Camera”.
